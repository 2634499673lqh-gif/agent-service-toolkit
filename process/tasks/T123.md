# T123 — In-process rate limiting and backpressure

**Model:** STANDARD; **Review:** Strong Review; **Depends:** T120, T121.

Bound expensive runtime/LLM work per service process with typed settings and fixed saturation
response. Preserve CRUD/auth/health availability, avoid retry loops, and expose bounded metrics/log
fields without secrets. This is not distributed rate limiting.

**Evidence:** deterministic concurrent saturation tests, release-after-failure/cancellation tests,
tenant/auth regression, and no impact on read-only routes.
