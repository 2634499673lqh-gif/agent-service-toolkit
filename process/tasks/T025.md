# T025 — Tenant authorization helper

Implementation Model: STANDARD  
Review Model: STRONG_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Implement one reusable server-side policy/helper for membership, role and resource-tenant checks. Keep business authorization out of route handlers.

## Depends on

T024.

## Acceptance

- Same-tenant permitted operations succeed according to the role policy.
- Cross-tenant, non-member, inactive-member, insufficient-role and forged-resource-tenant attempts are denied consistently (404 where the approved existence-hiding policy requires it).
- Approval reads/decisions and future task/knowledge/memory repositories have an explicit policy hook.
- Tests prove repository filters and helper checks cannot be bypassed by client IDs or claims.

The single cross-tenant lookup rule (403 or 404) is frozen by T020 for every endpoint and repository. Full pytest is required at the tenant-authorization gate.
