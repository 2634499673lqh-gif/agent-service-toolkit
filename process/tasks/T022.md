# T022 — User, membership and role model/migration

Implementation Model: STANDARD  
Review Model: STRONG_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Implement the approved User/Organization relationship and minimal role representation. Do not implement login or authorization dependencies.

## Depends on

T020 and T021 accepted schema/migration.

## Scope

User, Membership (if selected), and role fields/tables exactly as approved; password hash or external identity field without authentication behavior; email normalization/uniqueness; active state; UTC timestamps; explicit foreign keys and non-destructive cascade policy; repositories and focused constraints/migration tests.

## Acceptance

- Duplicate/normalized email behavior, inactive users, membership uniqueness and role constraints are tested.
- Tenant ownership is represented by the approved relationship and cannot be supplied by request data.
- Hashes and credentials never appear in serialization, logs, fixtures, or API responses.
- Migration remains independent of LangGraph internal tables and supports the approved fresh/rollback strategy.

If T020 selects Membership, this card must either be replaced by a separately bounded `T022A — Membership model/migration` card or include an explicitly named membership subtask; no implementer may guess. Verify fresh/existing upgrades, revision metadata/version, LangGraph coexistence, and downgrade policy.
